Smart Water Quality Monitoring & Analysis System

Maharashtra Institute of Technology, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra
Department of Computer Science and Design

An embedded and cloud-enabled environmental engineering system designed to continuously monitor, evaluate, and classify drinking water quality against international standards (WHO / BIS IS 10500:2012).

1. Problem Statement

Conventional laboratory water testing is time-intensive, expensive, and non-continuous. Delayed detection of chemical or biological contamination poses acute public health hazards. This project automates immediate, onsite telemetry and multi-parametric potability classification.

2. Core Methodology

Hardware sensors capture analog voltage potentials corresponding to Hydrogen Ion activity (pH), electrical conductivity of dissolved solids (TDS), and 90-degree scattered infrared optical light (Turbidity). Readings are digitized and evaluated by our weighted WQI analytical engine.

3. Web & Database Tier

Developed on Python Flask with an optimized SQLite persistence layer. Features role-based access control, cryptographic password hashing, dynamic Chart.js analytics, and automated PDF certification generated via ReportLab.

Water Quality Index (WQI) Algorithmic Model

The system implements the Weighted Arithmetic Water Quality Index method. Sub-indices \(q_i\) are computed for each parameter based on standard deviation from ideal drinking water benchmarks:

WQI = (0.35 × qpH) + (0.35 × qTDS) + (0.30 × qTurbidity)
  • Safe (80 – 100): Optimal physiological mineralization and optical clarity. Unconditionally fit for drinking.
  • Moderate (50 – 79): Borderline mineral or turbidity levels. Requires mechanical sediment filtration or boiling.
  • Unsafe (< 50 or severe breach): Exceeds permissible health limits. Heavy industrial/bacterial risk. Direct consumption hazardous.
