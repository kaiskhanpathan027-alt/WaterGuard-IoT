Key System Capabilities

Comprehensive Water Analytics

Designed according to rigorous engineering methodologies, linking sensory measurements with automated potability diagnosis.

Tri-Sensor Multi-Parametric Input

Accurate simultaneous assessment of Hydrogen Potential (pH), Total Dissolved Solids (TDS in mg/L), and Nephelometric Turbidity (NTU).

Automated Quality Classification

Algorithmic evaluation assigns Safe, Moderate, or Unsafe potability statuses with a computed 0-100 Water Quality Index (WQI).

Certified Downloadable PDF Reports

Instant generation of print-ready laboratory certificates equipped with WHO benchmark tables and remediation instructions.

Interactive Trend Visualizations

Dynamic time-series charts powered by Chart.js allow students and researchers to track water safety patterns over time.

WHO & BIS Benchmark Criteria

Monitored Water Parameters

Understanding the physiological and physical significance of the monitored parameters.

1. pH Level (0 - 14)

Target: 6.5 – 8.5

Measures acidity or basicity of water. Water below 6.5 is acidic and corrosive, dissolving toxic heavy metals from pipes. Water above 8.5 is alkali, causing incrustation and bitter taste.

Sensor Used: E-201-C Industrial Glass Bulb pH Electrode with BNC Interface

2. Total Dissolved Solids

Limit: < 500 mg/L

Represents cumulative minerals, salts, and metals dissolved in water. Elevated TDS (>900 mg/L) causes renal load, pipe clogging, and severe gastrointestinal discomfort.

Sensor Used: Dual Titanium Pin Analog Electrical Conductivity (EC/TDS) Probe

3. Turbidity (NTU)

Limit: ≤ 5.0 NTU

Measures relative water clarity caused by suspended matter. Turbid water harbors pathogens, viruses, and shields bacteria from ultraviolet and chlorine purification.

Sensor Used: Infrared Optical Turbidity Sensor (TSD-10 / DF-Robot Gravity)
